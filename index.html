The obvious questions

Answered before you ask.

The seven questions we hear on every first call. Answered here so the second call is about your panel.

What is the difference between LT and HT panels?

LT panels handle voltages up to 1000V for distribution inside factories, hospitals and buildings. HT panels handle 11kV and 33kV switchgear for incoming feeders and grid substations. We build both, tested to IEC 61439 with up to 65kA short circuit rating.

Do you build custom sized panels?

Every panel is sized to the room, the load and the ambient. Send an SLD or a load list, back comes a costed BOM, breaker coordination and a short circuit study before any sheet is cut.

What standards do you build to?

IEC 61439 for LT and PCC/MCC panels, Form 3b and Form 4a on request. Tinned copper busbar, powder coated CRCA sheet, IP42 to IP54 ingress. HT switchgear to relevant IEC standards for 11kV and 33kV.

Do you deliver panels outside Karachi?

Yes. Panels are bench built, meggered and function tested in Karachi, torn down for delivery, then rechecked and commissioned on site anywhere in Pakistan.

Do you provide after sales support and PPM?

Yearly preventive maintenance, thermal scans, retorques and emergency callout in Karachi. The phone that rings is one of the three owners, not a call queue.

What industrial cable sizes do you stock?

Copper and aluminium, 1.5 to 630 sq mm. XLPE, PVC and LSZH insulation. Power, control, instrumentation, armoured, unarmoured and flexible. Local and imported drums, cut to length with continuity tested.
